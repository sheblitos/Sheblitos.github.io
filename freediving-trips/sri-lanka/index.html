Sri Lanka

Freediving plus the whole island

The water is only one part of the journey: south-coast diving, surf, wildlife, tea country and inland culture in one trip.

Mirissa gives access to warm Indian Ocean water and healthy reef. Whale-watching trips run seasonally from the south coast; sightings are never guaranteed and responsible operators matter. Trip timing follows the monsoon cycle.

  • Warm Indian OceanReef freediving from Mirissa
  • Seasonal whalesWith responsible operators
  • SurfBreaks for every level
  • YalaLeopards and elephants
  • EllaWaterfalls and tea country
  • SigiriyaThe rock fortress at sunrise

The journey

Stop by stop

Stop 01Mirissa & the south coast

Your base for the first days: whale watching, warm water, reef and surf.

Stop 02Yala

The great wild park at dawn: leopards, elephants and sloth bears.

Stop 03Ella

Waterfall hikes, the Nine Arch Bridge and the blue train through the tea.

Stop 04Nuwara Eliya

Tea estates at altitude, cool air and endless green.

Stop 05Sigiriya

The rock fortress, climbed at first light.

Private driver · full logistics airport to airport · local partners for freediving, surf school and whale watching.
