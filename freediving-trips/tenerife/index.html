Freediving in Tenerife

Tenerife is one of the easiest ocean destinations to combine with a European trip: Atlantic depth, volcanic landscapes and a strong year-round marine environment.

Atlantic depth training

Tenerife offers established freediving communities and access to deep Atlantic water, with conditions that differ clearly from the Mediterranean and Red Sea.

Resident cetaceans

Short-finned pilot whales and bottlenose dolphins are resident in waters around southwest Tenerife, making authorised whale-watching a major island experience.

Ocean plus volcanic landscape

A Tenerife trip can combine freediving with Teide, the Anaga mountains and coastal exploration, which makes it work well for mixed groups who want more than a training camp.

Wildlife expectations

Resident populations improve the chances of seeing cetaceans, but these are wild animals and no individual sighting can be guaranteed.

Marine-life activities should use authorised operators and follow local conservation rules. Wildlife sightings depend on nature, not a schedule.
