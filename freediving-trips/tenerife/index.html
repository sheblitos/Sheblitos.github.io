The experience

More than a freediving course

This isn't a classroom holiday with a beach attached. Each day mixes structured freediving education with real ocean time, island exploration and a group that gets close fast.

You train with an AIDA Instructor Trainer in a small group, so the coaching follows your level and your goals, whether that's your first breath-hold in open water or finding calm at depth. Around the training, Tenerife does the rest: Atlantic water, volcanic coastline and long dinners with people who love the ocean as much as you do.

Before you fly

Your adventure starts before takeoff

If you're based in Barcelona, we meet before the trip for a dedicated preparation day. Theory and pool work happen at home, so your days in Tenerife go to the ocean.

You receive the theory materials before the preparation day. The session follows the applicable AIDA standards, so depending on your course, some theory, pool requirements or prerequisites may need more than one session. We'll tell you exactly what applies to you.

Canary Islands · Atlantic

Why Tenerife

Four hours from most of Europe, with conditions that feel nothing like the Mediterranean or the Red Sea.

Atlantic depth

Deep Atlantic water close to shore, and established freediving communities on the island. A great place to train depth in a new environment.

Volcanic landscapes

Teide, the Anaga mountains and a dramatic coastline. Tenerife works for groups who want more than a training camp.

Marine life

Short-finned pilot whales and bottlenose dolphins are resident in the waters around southwest Tenerife. They are wild animals, so no sighting can be guaranteed.

Marine-life activities use authorised operators and follow local conservation rules.

Community

Train and explore with people who share your passion for the ocean. The friendships usually outlast the trip.
