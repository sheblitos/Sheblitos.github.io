Indonesia

Freediving plus adventure

Bali combines serious freediving infrastructure with reef diving, warm water and easy access to a much wider Indonesia journey.

Amed on Bali's northeast coast is one of the island's best-known freediving bases, with calm-water days and depth close to shore. Manta rays and other pelagic wildlife may be encountered, but sightings depend on season and conditions and are never guaranteed.

  • AmedCalm water and depth close to shore
  • USAT Liberty wreck30 m of WWII steel covered in reef
  • Gili AirTurtles on every dive
  • LiveaboardFour days through rich water
  • KomodoPadar Island and Pink Beach
  • Local partnersTrusted schools at every stop

The journey

Stop by stop

Stop 01Ubud

Start here. Temples, rice terraces, morning markets and good food.

Stop 02Amed

Freediving on the quiet northeast coast with Ocean Prana Bali, ten minutes from the USAT Liberty wreck.

Stop 03Gili Air

No motorbikes, no noise, no rush: diving with Pacific Freedive Academy.

Stop 04The liveaboard

Four days aboard with Dewi through the richest water of the trip.

Stop 05Komodo & Labuan Bajo

Padar Island at sunrise, Pink Beach and Komodo dragons on Rinca.

Local partners: Ocean Prana Bali (Amed) · Pacific Freedive Academy (Gili Air) · Dewi (liveaboard). Full logistics between every stop.
