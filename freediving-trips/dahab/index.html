Red Sea · Sinai

Where freediving meets adventure

Dahab is a small Sinai town where deep water starts a few fin kicks from the shore. Freedivers from around the world have trained here for years, and once you see it, it's easy to understand why.

You walk in from the beach to established training sites, dive along coral walls that drop into deep blue, and come back to a waterfront full of freedivers from every corner of the world. Behind the town, the Sinai mountains turn red at sunset, and the desert is a short drive away. The Red Sea stays around 24°C all year.

  • Deep water from the shoreNo long boat rides to reach depth
  • A real freediving townEstablished schools, coaches and buddies
  • The Blue HoleOne of the Red Sea's most famous sites
  • Coral reefsColour and life on every dive
  • Mountains and desertSouth Sinai above the surface
  • Bedouin hospitalityTea, food and a slower pace of life

Why Dahab, for me

Dahab is where my freediving life began. I came for a short break, did my first course and stayed. I taught and coached here for years, as an instructor and then as an AIDA Instructor Trainer. On this trip you discover Dahab with someone who knows its sites, its conditions and its freediving community.

  4. DAY4

    Blue Hole experienceHighlight

    Discover Dahab's iconic freediving location

    A day at the Blue Hole, one of the most recognisable places in the underwater world: a deep blue circle in the reef, a few metres from the shore, under the Sinai mountains.

    • Guided site orientation
    • Recreational freediving suited to your level
    • Marine-life observation
    • Underwater photos and video
    • Optional supervised training
    • Relaxing on the coast

    Safety first. We stay in open water inside the Blue Hole and along the reef. We never swim through the Arch, tunnels or any overhead environment. Every session has supervision, a safety plan and emergency procedures, and activities match your experience, certification and the sea conditions.

  5. DAY5

    Ras Mohammed National ParkHighlight

    Explore the Red Sea's marine paradise

    Morning

    • Early departure from Dahab
    • Park orientation and environmental briefing
    • Exploring selected marine sites

    In the water

    • Guided snorkelling
    • Recreational freediving at suitable sites
    • Coral reef environments and marine life
    • Underwater photos and video

    Afternoon and evening

    • Lunch and time to relax
    • More exploring if conditions and logistics allow
    • Return to Dahab, free time and a relaxed group dinner

    Good to know. Activities depend on sea conditions, park rules and your experience. We protect the reef: we look, we never touch. Wildlife is wild, so no sighting can be guaranteed.

South Sinai · Egypt

Why Dahab

Freediving brought Dahab its reputation. The desert, the people and the pace of life are why people keep coming back.

TrainingLighthouse

A well-established freediving site with easy shore access, right by the town. Where most of the week's training happens.

IconBlue Hole

One of Dahab's most recognisable underwater places, where deep blue water meets the reef a few metres from the shore.

Protected reefRas Mohammed

A national park known for its coral reefs and the diversity of Red Sea life. Sightings vary, as they always do with wild animals.

Above the surfaceSinai landscapes

Mountains, desert and dramatic coastline. The scenery changes colour through the day and turns red at sunset.

CommunityDahab's freedivers

An international community of freedivers, coaches and ocean lovers. Buddies are easy to find here.

CultureSinai hospitality

A relaxed beach town, Bedouin tea and food, and a pace of life that slows you down in the best way.
