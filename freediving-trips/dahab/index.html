Freediving in Dahab, Egypt

Dahab is where Freediving Brain began: deep water close to shore, a long-established freediving community and the Red Sea landscape that makes training feel inseparable from the place.

Depth close to shore

Dahab is unusually practical for depth training because deep water is accessible from the coast at several well-known sites.

A real freediving community

The town has long attracted freedivers from around the world, which makes it easy to combine focused training with a strong buddy culture.

The Blue Hole and The Bells

The Blue Hole is one of Dahab’s most famous diving sites. Freedivers can experience dramatic reef walls and deep blue water, but site choice and depth should always match training level, conditions and local safety planning.

Lighthouse

Lighthouse is a central Dahab training area used by many freedivers because of its access, depth profile and proximity to the town.

Ras Mohammed

Trips from Dahab can also include Red Sea exploration farther south, combining freediving with reef scenery and a different environment from daily line training.

Freediving Brain trips are experience-led rather than depth-chasing. Certified divers and beginners are briefed according to their level, and open-water activities depend on conditions.
