Our Story

The people behind Barcelona Freedivers

Met in the water. Built a school.

It started in Dahab, on the Red Sea. We met in the water, fell in love, and built a freediving school.

Shebl & Rimi

Shebl

Founder & Head Instructor Trainer

The vibe: Inner calm & psychological mastery

An AIDA Instructor Trainer, the highest teaching level in AIDA, Shebl teaches every level, from beginner courses to the AIDA Instructor Course, and has certified more than 160 freedivers. He is also an AIDA Freediving Judge at competitions.

Freediving since 2021 and teaching since 2022, he spent five years in Dahab on the Red Sea before bringing Barcelona Freedivers to Barcelona. On every Barcelona Freedivers trip, in Dahab, Sri Lanka and Indonesia, he is in the water leading the dives.

Read Shebl’s instructor profile and guides →

Rimi

Assistant Instructor, AIDA Judge & Trip Organiser

The vibe: High-energy spark & architect of the journey

An Architect and Product Design Lead by profession, Rimi is the first and deepest Iraqi female freediver. An Assistant Instructor and AIDA Freediving Judge, she is also a world traveller who has lived in six countries and explored more than 45. At Barcelona Freedivers, she is the architect behind every trip and adventure.

From Dahab to the world

We've dived with whales, mantas and dolphins around the world. Now based in Barcelona, teaching, and running freediving trips.
