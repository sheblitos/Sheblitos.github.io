From Dahab to Barcelona

How it started

Shebl built his freediving teaching experience in Dahab, on the Red Sea, in one of the sport’s best-known depth-training environments. After certifying more than 160 freedivers across every level, he brought Barcelona Freedivers to Barcelona, combining recognised AIDA education with a strong mental-performance framework.

Teaching philosophy

Calm is a technical skill

The goal is not to make a student fight harder for metres. It is to make technique, safety, equalisation and mental state clear enough that progression becomes repeatable.

Areas of focus

What he teaches

  • AIDA education from beginner to instructor
  • Equalisation, Frenzel and advanced depth technique
  • Safety, rescue and buddy systems
  • Breath-hold physiology and the dive reflex
  • Mental performance and nervous-system regulation
  • Pool and open-water training
