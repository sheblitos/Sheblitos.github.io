Good to know

Course questions

Do I need any experience?

Not for the Beginner course. It is made for complete beginners: you only need to be comfortable in the water. Each next level requires the one before it.

Where do the courses take place?

In Barcelona: pool sessions first, then open-water dives in the Mediterranean.

Are AIDA certificates recognised worldwide?

Yes. AIDA certificates are accepted at freediving schools, dive sites and liveaboards worldwide, and they do not expire.

I already have a certificate from another school. Can I continue here?

Yes. AIDA certificates are recognised internationally, so you start from your next level. Certified freedivers can also join our training sessions.
