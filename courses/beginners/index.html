What you'll learn

In this course

  • Breathing and relaxation before every dive
  • Longer, calmer breath-holds in the pool
  • Equalisation: clear your ears comfortably as you go down
  • Finning and technique that save oxygen
  • Safety, rescue and the buddy system
  • Calm, guided open-water dives around 20 m

How it works

Pool first. Then the sea.

  1. Day 1 · AIDA 1 in the poolBreathing, relaxation and your first calm breath-holds. You feel the dive reflex and learn how freedivers keep each other safe.
  2. AIDA 2 · pool and theoryEqualisation, efficient finning, rescue and buddy skills, practised calmly in the pool before you head out.
  3. AIDA 2 · the MediterraneanGuided open-water dives, building calmly towards around 20 m at your own pace.

Good to know

Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. This course is made for complete beginners. You only need to be comfortable in the water.

Can I book AIDA 1 or AIDA 2 on its own?

No. AIDA 1 and AIDA 2 are always taught together as one course, so you learn in the right order: pool first, then the sea.

How deep will I go?

The course works towards dives of around 20 m, always at your own pace and comfort.

Is equipment included?

Yes, all equipment is included in the course price.
