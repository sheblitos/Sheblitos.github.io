Course questions

Do I need freediving experience for AIDA 1?

No. AIDA 1 is designed as an introduction to freediving for people who are comfortable in the water but may have no previous breath-hold diving experience.

Is AIDA 1 pool only?

The Freediving Brain AIDA 1 format is a one-day introduction focused on theory, safety and pool-based skills.

What should I learn before going deeper?

Relaxation, basic breath-hold awareness, buddy safety and clean technique come before depth. AIDA 1 is designed to establish those foundations.
