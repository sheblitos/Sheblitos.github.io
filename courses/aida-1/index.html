Why train with Barcelona Freedivers?

Courses are led through a small-group coaching model focused on calm, clear feedback and safe progression. Founder Shebl is an AIDA Instructor Trainer and Judge, with years of teaching experience from Dahab to Barcelona.

Course questions

Do I need freediving experience for AIDA 1?

No. AIDA 1 is designed as an introduction to freediving for people who are comfortable in the water but may have no previous breath-hold diving experience.

Is AIDA 1 pool only?

The Barcelona Freedivers AIDA 1 format is a one-day introduction focused on theory, safety and pool-based skills.

What should I learn before going deeper?

Relaxation, basic breath-hold awareness, buddy safety and clean technique come before depth. AIDA 1 is designed to establish those foundations.
