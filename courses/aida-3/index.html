Good to know

Questions

What changes from AIDA 2 to AIDA 3?

AIDA 3 develops deeper open-water technique, freefall, more advanced equalisation, stronger safety skills and more independent training awareness.

Do I need AIDA 2 first?

Yes. AIDA 3 requires AIDA 2 or a recognised equivalent certification.

Is mouthfill part of deeper freediving?

Mouthfill is an advanced equalisation strategy used by deeper freedivers. Whether and when it is introduced depends on your Frenzel control, depth needs and readiness.
