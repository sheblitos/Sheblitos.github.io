Course questions

What changes from AIDA 2 to AIDA 3?

AIDA 3 develops deeper open-water technique, freefall, more advanced equalization, stronger safety skills and more independent training awareness.

Do I need AIDA 2 first?

Yes. AIDA 3 is an advanced course and requires the appropriate prior freediving certification or recognised equivalent according to the applicable course standards.

Is mouthfill part of deeper freediving?

Mouthfill is an advanced equalization strategy used by deeper freedivers. Whether and when it is introduced depends on the diver’s current Frenzel control, depth needs and readiness.
