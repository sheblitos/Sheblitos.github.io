Course questions

Do I need AIDA 1 before AIDA 2?

Freediving Brain accepts beginners into AIDA 2 when they meet the course entry requirements and are comfortable in the water; AIDA 1 can also be used as a shorter introduction first.

How deep is AIDA 2 training?

The course introduces open-water depth training around the 20 metre range, with progression based on technique, comfort and the required course standards.

What is the most important skill in AIDA 2?

Safety and reliable equalization matter more than chasing depth. The course combines buddy procedures, rescue, Frenzel development, finning and controlled open-water technique.
