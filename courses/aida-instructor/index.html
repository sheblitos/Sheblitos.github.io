What you’ll learn

Freediving Brain combines the AIDA curriculum with a mind-first approach: technique, safety and physiology are taught alongside relaxation, attention and nervous-system regulation.

Training in Barcelona

Sessions are based in Barcelona, using pool training for controlled skill development and Mediterranean open water where the course level requires depth work. Equipment is included according to the course format shown on our main Courses page.

AIDA Instructor at a glance

  • Teaching AIDA 1–3 skills and theory
  • AIDA standards and course structure
  • Safety leadership and problem solving
  • Supervised teaching practice
  • Feedback and instructor development
  • Mentoring with AIDA Instructor Trainer Shebl

Why train with Freediving Brain?

Courses are led through a small-group coaching model focused on calm, clear feedback and safe progression. Founder Shebl is an AIDA Instructor Trainer and Judge, with years of teaching experience from Dahab to Barcelona.

Course questions

Who should apply for the AIDA Instructor Course?

The programme is for experienced freedivers who meet the relevant AIDA prerequisites and are ready to move from performing skills to teaching, evaluating and managing safety.

How long is the instructor programme?

Freediving Brain’s instructor pathway is structured as a 10–21 day mentored programme, depending on the candidate, schedule and teaching-practice plan.

Who leads the instructor training?

The programme is led by Shebl, an AIDA Instructor Trainer and Judge, with a small-cohort mentoring approach and supervised teaching practice.
