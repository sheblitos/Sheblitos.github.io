Good to know

Questions

Who should apply?

Experienced freedivers who meet the AIDA prerequisites and are ready to move from performing skills to teaching, evaluating and managing safety.

How long is the programme?

A 10–21 day mentored programme, depending on the candidate, schedule and teaching-practice plan.

Who leads the instructor training?

Shebl, an AIDA Instructor Trainer and Judge, with a small-cohort mentoring approach and supervised teaching practice.
