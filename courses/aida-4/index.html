Good to know

Questions

Who is AIDA 4 for?

Experienced freedivers who have AIDA 3 and want to develop advanced depth, rescue, training planning and self-coaching skills.

Is AIDA 4 only about reaching 40 metres?

No. Depth is only one part. Advanced rescue, technique, planning, equalisation and training intelligently are central to the Master Freediver level.

Does AIDA 4 prepare me for instructor training?

Yes. It builds the technical, safety and self-coaching foundation expected before instructor-level training.
