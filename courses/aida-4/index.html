Course questions

Who is AIDA 4 for?

AIDA 4 is for experienced freedivers who already have the prerequisite certification and want to develop advanced depth, rescue, training planning and self-coaching skills.

Is AIDA 4 only about reaching 40 metres?

No. Depth is only one part of the course. Advanced rescue, technique, planning, equalization and the ability to train intelligently are central to the Master Freediver level.

Does AIDA 4 prepare me for instructor training?

AIDA 4 is an important step toward the professional pathway because it develops the technical, safety and self-coaching foundation expected before instructor-level training.
