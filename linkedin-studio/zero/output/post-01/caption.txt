Before freediving, before Barcelona, there was a bag.

2014. I was in college with an idea: custom painted bags. The brand grew, people bought them, and a few blogs wrote about it. My first taste of building something from nothing.

A few years later I joined an agency in its first year as a business developer. No portfolio, no case studies, no big name. I helped land its first 10 clients, and created a lot of the content too.

Both taught me the same thing. At zero, nobody buys your product. They buy whether they believe you'll show up.

Logos and case studies come later. Trust comes first.

This is part 1 of a short series on everything I've built from zero.
