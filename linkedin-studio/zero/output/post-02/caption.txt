Part 2. The startup years. Fast, messy, and the best school I ever had.

During COVID I did B2B sales for a restaurant ordering and loyalty startup, while restaurants were fighting to stay open.

Then I became the first employee after the founder of a healthy meal delivery startup. Growth, SEO, paid ads, the website and ordering system, HubSpot, the chefs, operations, even accounting. When you're employee number one, there is no "not my job".

Then I built my own: a health and wellness marketplace for Egypt. I validated it with businesses, partnered with a software company, and pitched seed programs and angel investors. It wasn't funded.

It still taught me more than any success. You only learn what a business really needs when you've had to do every part of it yourself.

Next week: what all of this cost me.
