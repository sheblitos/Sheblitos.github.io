Most people in tech breathe like they're late for something.

Short, high, fast breaths. All day. You don't notice until someone points it out.

That's why our team sessions start on the mat, fully dry, before anyone gets near the water. Learning to slow the breath down on command.

Here's one you can try before your next hard meeting.
Breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds.
Breathe out slowly for 8.
Repeat for one minute.

The long exhale is the part that matters. It helped me through years of pressure, and I've seen it help many of my students.

One rule: breath-holds only with a trained instructor, never in or near water alone. Slow breathing on land is for everyone.

Try it today. What did you notice?
