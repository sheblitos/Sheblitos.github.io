The hardest exercise I give busy people is doing nothing.

Static is the simplest discipline in freediving. You float face down in shallow water, relaxed, on one breath, while your buddy stays right beside you, watching and keeping time.

For people who live in Slack, the first seconds are loud. The mind wants to check something, fix something, move. Then the urge to breathe arrives, and with it the real question: do you fight it, or do you relax into it?

The ones who relax go longer. Almost every time.

That's the lesson a team carries back to the office. When things get intense, the instinct is to do more. Often the better move is to slow down.

Always in shallow water, always with a trained instructor. Safety is the first thing everyone learns.

What would change in your team if everyone could stay calm for one more breath?
