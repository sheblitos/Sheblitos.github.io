Some of my best days in Dahab had nothing to do with depth.

In 2024 and 2025 I ran trips for the freediving school I worked with. Long days on the boat, people from different places sharing one deck.

What stays with me isn't the numbers anyone hit. It's moments like this one, everyone shouting at the camera at the end of the day.

People come for the water. They come back for the people.

I'm building that same feeling into everything in Barcelona now. The school, the team sessions, and EXTour, which started because finding the right people to dive with was always the hardest part.

Who are the people that keep you coming back?
