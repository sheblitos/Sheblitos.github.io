Every good dive starts on the beach.

Before my students touched the water, we stood on the sand and talked. What we're doing, how deep, which signals we use, what happens if something feels wrong.

A few minutes on land saved a lot of confusion in the water.

Most marketing skips this part. Someone says "let's run ads" and the campaign is live before anyone agreed on who it's for or what success looks like.

The brief isn't paperwork. It's the dive plan.
