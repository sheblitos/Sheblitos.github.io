Five years ago I took five days off in a small town by the Red Sea. I stayed five years.

Slowly, I built a life there. Teaching every day, students sending their friends, a strong name by word of mouth. I was even building my own school. Stable. Good. Really good.

And in the middle of all of it, I stopped.

Because I knew I'm here for something bigger. Not better than what I had. Bigger than staying comfortable.

So I left. Not just the town. My country. The comfort zone I spent five years building.

Now I'm in Barcelona, starting from zero again. But a stronger zero. I'm bringing everything with me: the marketer, the instructor, the founder.

The hardest part of a dive is leaving the surface. The light is right there, warm and familiar. You go anyway, because everything you came for is below.

So here I am, going down again. Building Freediving Brain and EXTour, and back in marketing with fresh skills.

If you're in Barcelona and work in wellness, sport, adventure or tech, I'd love to meet you.

What's the surface you know you need to leave, but haven't yet?
