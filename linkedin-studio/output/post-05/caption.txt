The dive that changed my life almost ended it.

Dahab, alone in the water, no course, no buddy. Just me thinking I knew enough.

I lost motor control at the surface. By pure luck, an instructor I knew was nearby. He got me back.

Everyone had told me: never dive alone. I finally understood why.

That day I signed up for my first course. A year later I was teaching.

I took the lesson out of the water too. Never build alone. Not a business, not a project, not a life.

Who's your buddy right now?
