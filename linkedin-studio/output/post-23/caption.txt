The quietest place I know is upside down on a line.

No phone. No notifications. No one asking for "a quick call". Just the line, the blue, and one breath.

You can't take anything with you down there. Not your inbox, not your to-do list, not the argument from this morning. Your mind either goes quiet, or the dive ends early.

For years I thought focus was something I'd find once things calmed down. They never did.

Freediving taught me that quiet isn't found. It's made. Now I try to build it into my days in Barcelona: fewer screens, one thing at a time.

Where do you go to get quiet?
