People ask me what freediving is really like. Not the depth numbers. The days.

So here it is. Swipe through.

Good vibes on the buoy before the first dive. Equalizing on the surface. Balloons, because training happens on land too. Personal bests on the watch, and the face that comes with them. Celebrations that last longer than the dives. Rituals that make no sense to anyone else. And the quiet moment when someone gets over a fear they've carried for years.

The part nobody tells you: most students leave as friends.

That's the energy I'm bringing to Barcelona.
