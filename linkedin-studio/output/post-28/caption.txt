In 2024 I became lead instructor at a freediving school in Dahab. Suddenly my job wasn't only my students. It was the instructors.

Leading a team of instructors taught me more about management than any title before it.

You can't be on every buoy. You can't watch every dive. What you can do is make sure everyone shares the same safety rules, the same briefing and the same standards. Then you trust them.

At the same time I was running the school's marketing and social media. Two jobs that look different, and both came down to one thing: getting a team to tell the same story.

Good leaders in the water don't dive for their team. They make sure nobody dives alone.

How do you keep standards consistent when you can't be everywhere?
