In a freediving competition, depth isn't the result.

I've judged from the surface. An athlete can reach their target and make it all the way back up, and the dive still doesn't count until they show they're okay, clean and in control, in front of the judges.

The deepest dive means nothing if you don't bring it back to the surface.

Work is the same. Ideas in your head, half-finished projects, a strategy nobody has seen. All depth, no result.

That's why it's now the last line of how I work: Go deep. Find the signal. Build the test. Bring it to the surface.

Ship it. Show it. Let people see it.

What are you sitting on that's ready to surface?
