The first time I freedived, something happened I still can't fully explain.

Everything aligned. Body, heart, mind and soul. For the first time in years, they were all in the same place at the same time.

Think about where you are underwater. A completely new environment with different rules. You don't breathe. Gravity works differently, so after a few meters you stop swimming and start flying. Nothing from your normal life follows you down there.

I came to the water burned out. I didn't know I was looking for this feeling until I found it.

That's why I call freediving a journey. You take it by yourself, for yourself, and you come back up a little more connected every time.

When was the last time everything in you felt aligned?
