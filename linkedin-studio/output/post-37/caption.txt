Every dive asks you to face something small. Some days it isn't small at all.

The first time you let go of the buoy. The first time you look down into blue with no bottom. The first time the urge to breathe arrives and you choose to stay calm instead of rushing up.

I've watched people face fears in the water they had carried for years. Fear of water, of losing control, of their own thoughts. Some told me afterwards it felt therapeutic. I can only share what I've seen: something changes when you overcome a fear with your whole body, not just your head.

And then you surface. The first breath, the laugh, the high five on the buoy. That feeling after a dive is one of the best I know, and every new personal best brings it back.

What's a fear you overcame that changed more than you expected?
