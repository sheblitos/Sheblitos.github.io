The most important decision in a deep dive happens on land.

Before every dive, I set my target depth. Then I stop negotiating.

Underwater, the mind starts bargaining. The urge to breathe gets louder, and "just two more meters" suddenly sounds reasonable. That's exactly how divers get hurt.

So the rule is simple: decide on land, not on the line.

I use the same rule at work now. Budget limits, launch dates, what I'll say no to. I set them when I'm calm, because a rushed mind makes rushed choices.

A calm mind decides. A rushed mind only reacts.

What's one decision you'd rather make before the deadline arrives?
