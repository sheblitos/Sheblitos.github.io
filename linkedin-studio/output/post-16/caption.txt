"Our classes are for everyone."

I see this line on small wellness brands all the time. It sounds generous. It's actually the problem.

When you talk to everyone, nobody feels you're talking to them.

The people who came to my freediving courses were never "everyone". Some were afraid of water. Some needed a break from stress. Some were athletes chasing depth. Each of them needed a different first sentence.

Pick one person. Name what's on their mind. Then show how you help.

Smaller message. Stronger signal.

Who is the one person your brand is really for?
