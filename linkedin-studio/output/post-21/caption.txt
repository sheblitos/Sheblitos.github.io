Going down is the easy part.

In freediving, the descent feels effortless. The sea pulls you down, you relax, you fall.

The way back up is where it gets real. Your legs are tired, the urge to breathe is loud, and the surface still looks far away. That's why good divers plan the ascent before they leave the surface. And why a buddy meets you on the way up.

I didn't plan mine in 2021. I went deep into work, into building, into pressure. I never thought about how I'd come back.

Now I do. Rest, sleep, people around me. They're not breaks from the work. They're the part of the dive that gets me home.

What's your plan for coming back up?
