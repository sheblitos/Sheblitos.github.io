Every team has one. The person who quietly dreads the "water part" of the offsite.

I've taught many people who were afraid of water. Some of the biggest moments I've seen in freediving came from them.

That's why every part of our team sessions has a dry version. Breathing, regulation and buddy work on land, with the same lessons, without getting wet. The water is an invitation, never a requirement.

Team building that leaves someone on the side isn't team building.

Who on your team would need the dry version, and would they ever tell you?
