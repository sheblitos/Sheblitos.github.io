Teaching freediving made me better at my job on land. Not the diving part. The people part.

160+ students, and every one arrived with something different. Fear, ambition, stress, curiosity. Here's what they taught me.

Reading people. Long before anyone says "I'm nervous", the breathing changes, the shoulders rise, the jokes get louder. You learn to notice.

Clear communication. A dive briefing has no room for vague words. Who goes first, what each signal means, what happens if something feels wrong. Short, clear, repeated.

Patience. Nobody relaxes because you tell them to. You wait, you adjust, you try another way.

Adapting. The exercise that works for one student blocks the next. You change the plan, not the person.

Feedback that lands. After a hard dive, a long list helps nobody. One clear thing to work on does.

Responsibility. When someone's safety depends on you, you stay present. Every second.

I use every one of these now, with clients, with teams and in every meeting.
