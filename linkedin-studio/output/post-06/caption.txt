In freediving, nobody dives alone. One person dives. One person watches. Then you switch.

The one watching isn't a spectator. They follow every second of the dive, ready to act if something goes wrong. The one diving has to let go and trust that they will.

Most team building talks about trust. Trust falls, ropes courses, a slide about psychological safety.

This is different. It's not a metaphor. Your colleague is underwater, and for that one breath you are responsible for them. Then they are responsible for you.

You don't forget the person who watched your dive.

Who on your team would you trust to watch yours?
