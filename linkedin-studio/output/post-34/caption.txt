In freediving, we never push through pressure. We equalize.

Every few meters, the water presses on your ears. You don't fight it. You make a small, gentle adjustment and it's gone. If it doesn't work, you don't force it. You turn around and come back up. Forcing it is how people get hurt.

I wish I'd known this in my startup years. I treated every problem like a wall to push through. More hours, more effort, more coffee.

Good teams work like good freedivers. They adjust early and often, before things hurt. They speak up the moment something doesn't feel right. And they're not ashamed to turn around when a plan isn't working.

Turning around isn't failing. It's how you get to dive again tomorrow.

Where is your team pushing through when it should be equalizing?
