70 meters. On one breath. 20% physical, 80% mental.

That's the deepest I've gone. Taller than a 20-storey building, straight down a line and back up again, with no tank.

People ask what it takes. The answer is aligning your body, mind and soul to work together.

The physical part is just training. The hard part is the mind. Staying quiet in a place with completely new rules. No breathing, and new gravity rules that let you free-fall down to the depth while you let go. Every thought you bring with you gets louder down there.

The soul. Knowing why you're going. On a deep dive you can't fake it. If you're there for ego, you feel it.

And here's what surprised me. Freediving shows you how you truly react to a new environment, to stress, to discomfort. Then it teaches you to meet them focused, relaxed and calm. Once you do it, it changes you deeply.

Going deep myself was one thing. Teaching others to do it was something else. You can't hand someone the depth recipe. You can only help them build their body, their calm and their reasons, and learn to align and connect body, mind and soul. Every dive is a new discovery.
