Why would a tech team learn to hold its breath?

Because most of us never leave the same environment. Same screens, same meetings, same notifications. The mind runs fast all day, and we breathe like we're late for something.

Freediving puts you somewhere completely new. Different rules. No breathing. Even gravity works differently. In that environment your mind shows you what it really does. It rushes, it doubts, it negotiates. And slowly you learn to let it go quiet.

You never do it alone. One person dives, one stays right next to them, guiding them to relax. Titles disappear. The only goal is to help each other reach the calmest place you can.

That's what a team takes back to work. A way to calm the mind when it speeds up, and real trust in the person next to them.
