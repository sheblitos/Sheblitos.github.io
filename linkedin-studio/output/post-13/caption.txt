The surface always looks brightest when you're leaving it.

When you start a dive and look up, the light is right there. Warm, easy, familiar. Every meter down, it gets a little further away.

I felt that leaving Dahab. Years of teaching, a team I led, a life I knew. I left it to start over in Barcelona, building my own school and an app from zero.

Some days I miss the light. But you don't find anything new by staying where it's bright.

What did you have to leave to start something real?
