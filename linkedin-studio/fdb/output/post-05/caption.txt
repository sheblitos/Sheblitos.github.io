A 60-second breathing reset you can use before your next meeting.

Sit tall. Shoulders soft.
Breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds.
Breathe out slowly through your mouth for 8 seconds.
Repeat for one minute.

The long exhale is the part that matters. Slowing the breath, especially the out-breath, is one of the simplest ways to tell your body it can calm down.

Freedivers do a version of this before every dive. Not to take in more air, but to arrive relaxed. Tension costs oxygen underwater, and at work it costs focus.

No breath-holds here. This one is just slow breathing, and it's safe to do anywhere. Breath-holds belong in a course, with a trained instructor and a buddy.

Try it once today. What did you notice?
