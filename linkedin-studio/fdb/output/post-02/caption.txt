Freediving is diving on one breath. No tanks, no bubbles, no noise.

Most people picture someone going as deep as they can. That's one part of it. Freedivers actually train in a few different disciplines.

Static: floating face down in a pool, completely still, on one breath. It's the purest training for the mind.
Dynamic: swimming horizontally underwater along the pool, with or without fins.
Depth: diving down a line in the sea. With fins, without fins, or pulling yourself down the rope, like in this photo. That last one is called free immersion.

What connects all of them is relaxation. Tension burns oxygen. The calmer you are, the further you go. That's why freediving is as much mental training as physical.

And in every discipline, there's always a buddy watching from close by.
