What actually happens in a freediving team workshop?

People expect us to throw their team into the deep end. It's the opposite.

We start on the mat, fully dry. Breathing exercises, relaxation, and the first experience of calming the body on purpose. The same tool everyone will use for the rest of the day.

Then the pool, in pairs. One person does a relaxed breath-hold. The other stays right beside them, watching and guiding them to relax. Then they switch. Titles and departments disappear fast when someone is responsible for you.

For groups who want the full experience, a guided session in open water. Then we sit down together: what happened in your body, and how to use it at work on Monday.

No experience needed. Non-swimmers are fully included, because every part has a dry version.

Formats run from a 2 to 4 hour workshop in Barcelona to full days and retreats.
