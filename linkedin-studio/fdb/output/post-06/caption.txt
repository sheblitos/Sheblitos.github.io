We organize freediving and breathwork experiences for companies and startups.

Here's how it works.

You tell us about your team. How many people, how much time, and what you want them to take back to work. Calmer under deadlines, better communication, more trust, or a day they'll talk about for months.

Then we design the format around you.
A 2 to 4 hour workshop in Barcelona, out of the water: breathwork, regulation, partner exercises.
A half or full day with the pool, and open water for groups who want the full experience.
A multi-day retreat in Barcelona or Dahab.

No experience needed. Non-swimmers are fully included, because every part has a dry version. Every session is led in person by our founder Shebl, an AIDA Instructor Trainer, with strict buddy safety and small groups.

Planning your next offsite? Send us a message or visit freedivingbrain.com, and we'll design it together, with no commitment.
