In the water, the CEO and the newest hire are equals.

Same wetsuit. Same mask. Same single breath.

When we run team sessions, job titles stop mattering within the first hour. The person keeping you safe might report to you on Monday. The person relaxing on the surface might be the one who usually makes every decision.

That changes things. Leaders who go first, admit they're a bit nervous, and let a junior colleague guide them through a breath-hold show their team something no offsite speech can.

It's not about the depth. It's about seeing each other differently when you come back up.

What would change in your team if everyone met as equals for one day?
