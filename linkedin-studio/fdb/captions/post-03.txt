Why would a fast-moving team learn to freedive?

Think about a normal week in a startup or a corporate team. Deadlines, launches, back-to-back calls, messages at midnight. Most people spend the whole week breathing short and fast, reacting instead of deciding, and never really switching off.

Freediving puts people somewhere completely new, with different rules. No breathing. Gravity works differently. In that environment, everyone sees how they truly react to stress and discomfort, and then learns to meet it focused, relaxed and calm.

And nobody does it alone. One person holds their breath, the other stays right beside them, guiding them to relax. Titles disappear. The only goal is to help the person in front of you reach the calmest place they can.

That's what teams take back to work. A simple way to calm the nervous system when things speed up, clearer communication, and trust they've actually felt.
