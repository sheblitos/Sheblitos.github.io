Thinking about trying freediving? Here's what your first course looks like.

AIDA 1 is a one-day introduction. No freediving experience needed, you just need to be comfortable in the water.

You'll learn the basics of how breath-holding really works, including the mammalian dive reflex and why the urge to breathe isn't what most people think. You'll practise relaxation, your first relaxed breath-holds, and buddy safety in a calm pool.

Most people are surprised by two things. How much longer they can hold their breath once they relax. And how calm they feel afterwards.

At Freediving Brain we teach in small groups, with a mind-first approach: technique, safety and physiology, alongside relaxation and attention.

Courses run in Barcelona, in the pool and in the Mediterranean as you progress.
