Every human body has a built-in freediving mode.

It's called the mammalian dive reflex. When you hold your breath and your face touches cold water, a few things start happening on their own.

Your heart rate slows down.
Blood moves away from your arms and legs toward your core, where your heart and brain need it most.
Your body starts using oxygen more carefully.

Whales, seals and dolphins have it too. Ours is weaker, but it's there, and training makes you more aware of it.

It's one of the first things we teach in an AIDA 1 course, because it changes how people see breath-holding. You're not fighting your body. Your body already knows how to do this.

Did you know your body could do this?
