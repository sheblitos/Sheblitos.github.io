Rule number one in freediving: never dive alone.

Not "be careful". Not "only if you're experienced". Never.

Every dive has a buddy. One person dives, the other watches from the surface, ready to meet them on the way up. Then they switch. One up, one down, always.

When the diver surfaces, there's a short surface protocol. Breathe, show the OK sign, say "I'm OK". The buddy keeps watching for a few more seconds, because the moments right after a dive matter most.

This is what makes freediving one of the safest ways to explore the ocean when it's done right. Not the depth, not the equipment. The person watching you.

In our courses, buddy skills and rescue are taught before anyone goes deep. And it's why we built a Barcelona community: nobody should need to train alone.
