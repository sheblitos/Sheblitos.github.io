Freediving Brain is now in Barcelona.

We're a freediving school built around one idea: the mind goes first. Technique, safety and physiology matter, and we teach them properly. But what changes people is learning to relax, pay attention and calm their own nervous system, one breath at a time.

Here's what we do.
AIDA courses, from your very first breath-hold up to instructor level.
Pool training for certified freedivers, static and dynamic.
Team experiences for companies and startups, from a 2 to 4 hour workshop to multi-day retreats.
Freediving trips to Dahab, Bali, Sri Lanka and Tenerife.
A Barcelona community, so nobody ever has to train alone.

Our founder, Shebl, is an AIDA Instructor Trainer and Judge, with 160+ certified freedivers and five years of teaching behind him.
