## Language rules learned along the way
- Never "how to deal with pressure". Freedivers equalize with pressure; if it doesn't work, they turn back. Talk instead about the mind in a new environment with different rules.
- Breathing photos are shown in true colors, no dark shading (`"pure": true`).
- Facts: 160+ certified freedivers, 5 years in the water, over 1,000 coaching and training sessions.
- First dive: everything aligned, body, heart, mind and soul. New environment, no breathing, gravity changes, you fly.
- Many students came to freediving after burnout. Skills work on land, in a pool, in the sea, even with a bowl of water.
- Never claim freediving treats anything. "It helped me, and I've seen it help others."
