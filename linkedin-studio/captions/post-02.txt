Your team has spent hundreds of hours in meetings together. They've never seen each other hold their breath.

That sounds strange until you try it.

A single breath underwater strips everything away. No laptop, no title, no clever answer. Just how you respond when the pressure rises. Panic, and the air goes fast. Stay calm, and you find more than you thought you had.

In tech and startups, pressure is the normal state. Fast decisions, constant notifications, shallow breathing all day. Most team training works on skills and strategy. Almost none works on the state underneath them.

That's what freediving trains. The water is just the classroom.

Over the next weeks I'll share what happens when a team breathes, holds and dives together. Trust, competition, leadership, fear.
