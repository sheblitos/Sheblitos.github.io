I spent 2021 selling healthy living.

I was the least healthy person in the room.

Growth for a meal startup by day. My own wellness marketplace by night. Investor pitches, rejections, freelance work in between. No sleep, injuries, then COVID on top.

From the outside it looked like ambition. From the inside it felt like nothing.

So I took five days off in Dahab. I never came back to that life.

The drive was never the problem. Ignoring myself was.

I still build. I just don't disappear while doing it anymore.
