Your body has a calm-down button most people never use. All you need is a bowl of cold water.

Every human has the mammalian dive reflex. When cold water touches your face and you hold your breath, your heart rate slows down. It's the same reflex that helps freedivers stay relaxed underwater, and you carry it everywhere.

This is how I use it on land, on hard days.
Fill a bowl with cold water.
Breathe slowly for a minute, with long exhales.
Put your face in for a few relaxed seconds, then come up and breathe normally.

No app, no subscription. One of the oldest reflexes we have.

It's what I go back to when I feel my nervous system speeding up. Not a cure for anything. Just a reset I trust.

Keep it short and easy, sitting safely, and never hold your breath alone in water deeper than a bowl.
