Five years ago, I took five days off in a small town by the sea. I stayed five years.

Dahab gave me what I was missing. The ocean, a slower life, freediving (a new hobby that turned into a career), 160+ certified students, a team, a community and a really beautiful life.

Now I've left. Not just the town. My country, and my comfort zone.
I know I'm here for something bigger.

I'm in Barcelona. A metropolitan city, fast and full of energy, the opposite of where I've been. I moved here on purpose, to find out what my full potential looks like when I bring everything together. The marketer, the instructor, the entrepreneur.

Freediving taught me something about moments like this. The hardest part of a dive is leaving the surface. The light is right there, warm and familiar, and every meter down it gets a little further away. You go anyway, because everything you came for is below.

So here I am, going down again. Building my school, Freediving Brain, and EXTour, and back in marketing with fresh skills, stronger than before.

If you're in Barcelona and work in wellness, sport, adventure or tech, I'd love to meet you.
