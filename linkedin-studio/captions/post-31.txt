5 years in the water. 160+ certified freedivers. More than 1,000 coaching and training sessions.

Those are the numbers. They're not what I remember.

I remember first breath-holds that ended in a big smile. People who arrived nervous in the water and left asking when they could come back. Quiet ones who surprised everyone, including themselves.

Teaching taught me that every student brings something into the water. Fear, stress, old stories. My job was never just technique. It was making the water feel safe enough for them to let it go.

Many of them are still my friends today.

To everyone who trusted me with their breath: thank you. You taught me more than I taught you.

Who's the teacher who changed how you see something?
