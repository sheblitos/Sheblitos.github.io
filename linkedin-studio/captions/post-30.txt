Many of the people I've taught didn't come to freediving for the sport. They came after burnout.

Corporate jobs, startups, the fast life that slowly takes everything. They had tried to switch off and couldn't. The mind kept running, even on holiday.

What they find in freediving is simple, and it isn't magic. Breathing exercises that slow the body down. Breath-holds that teach you to stay with your thoughts instead of running from them. A place where you have to relax, because tension costs oxygen.

The best part is that the skills don't stay in the water. On land, in a pool, in the sea, even with a bowl of cold water at home, you can come back to them. When the nervous system starts speeding up again, you already know the way down.

It helped me after my own burnout, and I've seen it help many others.

If you've been through burnout, what actually helped you switch off?
