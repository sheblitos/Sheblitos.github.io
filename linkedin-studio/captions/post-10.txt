The deepest part of a dive is the part where I stop swimming.

After the first meters, the body gets heavier and the sea starts pulling you down. Freedivers call it freefall. You stop kicking, relax, and let the depth come to you.

Beginners fight it. They keep finning because stopping feels like losing control, and it costs them oxygen they need later.

I see the same thing in marketing. A campaign goes live and the ads get changed every few hours. Nothing gets the time to work.

Do the work at the start. Then trust it long enough to see what it does.

Where are you still swimming when you could be falling?
