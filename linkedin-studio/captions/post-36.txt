In our team sessions, nobody holds their breath for themselves.

We work in pairs. One person does a static breath-hold, completely still. The other stays right beside them. Not timing them like a coach, but guiding them. A calm voice. A light touch on the back. Helping them relax a little deeper.

Then you switch.

Years of buddy work taught me one thing: it only works if the care is real. People can feel the difference between a buddy who is checking a box and one who honestly cares. If the care isn't genuine, the trust doesn't happen, and nobody relaxes.

Titles disappear in that moment. The only goal is to help the person in front of you reach the calmest place they've ever been.

It starts on dry land, on a mat like this one, long before anyone touches the water.

When did someone at work last help you slow down instead of speed up?
