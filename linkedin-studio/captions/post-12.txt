Put people in a pool for dynamic and it becomes a race within minutes.

Dynamic is swimming horizontally underwater on one breath. Everyone wants to know how far the others went.

Then they discover the twist. The harder you push, the faster your air runs out. The ones who kick like their life depends on it stop first. The relaxed ones glide past them.

I've watched it happen again and again in the pool. The conversation changes. "How far did you go?" becomes "How did you stay so calm?" People start coaching each other.

That's the healthy kind of competition. The kind where everyone gets better because someone else did.

How does competition show up in your team right now?
