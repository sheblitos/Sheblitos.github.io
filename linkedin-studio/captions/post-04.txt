160+ students taught me more about marketing than any course.

Every person who came to dive brought something with them. Fear of water. Stress. Anxiety. Old stories they didn't share at first.

None of them cared about technique until they felt safe.

Marketing works the same way. People don't buy features. They buy the feeling that they're understood.

Most brands start talking before they start listening. The ocean taught me to do it the other way around.
