Not every dive needs a target.

Some days I train for depth. One line, one number, full focus.

Other days I just explore. A reef canyon, no plan, following whatever looks interesting. Some of my favorite moments in the water came from those dives.

Both matter. The mistake is not knowing which one you're on.

I build the same way now. Some projects have a clear goal and a deadline. Others are small experiments where I'm just curious what happens. That's often where new ideas show up.

Decide before you go in: is this a depth day or an exploring day?

What are you exploring right now, with no target yet?
