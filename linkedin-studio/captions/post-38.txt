People see a freediver gliding down a line and think it's all about calm. That's half the story.

Freediving is a full athletic sport. Fin technique, chest and diaphragm flexibility, equalization skills, leg strength, months of small improvements. You train the body like any athlete. And you train the mind at the same time, because a tense body burns oxygen fast.

Neither works alone. A strong diver with a busy mind won't go far. A calm diver with poor technique won't either.

That's how I think about growth at work too. You can't only work on mindset, and you can't only work on skills. The people who grow fastest train both, patiently, one small improvement at a time.

Every personal best I've seen came from that. Not one big leap. Hundreds of small ones.

Which do you train more right now, the skills or the mind?
