Good to know

Questions before you go

Do I need to be a certified freediver?

No. Trips welcome complete beginners, experienced freedivers and anyone comfortable in the water. Shebl briefs every dive and keeps everyone at a depth that feels right.

What's included?

Guided freediving with Shebl on every dive, all dive equipment, accommodation and trusted local operators at each stop, and curated land adventures.

Who leads the trips?

Shebl and Rimi personally. Shebl is in the water on every dive; Rimi runs the journey end to end.

Are the trips safe?

Yes. Every session runs with full safety protocols, buddy diving and emergency oxygen on site, with Shebl in the water with the group.

How do I get a spot?

Tap Book now and choose your trip. Dates go to the booking list first, and small groups fill quickly.
