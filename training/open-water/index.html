Mediterranean · Depth training

Open-water freediving training in Barcelona

Depth sessions for certified freedivers who want to refine equalization, freefall, line technique and mental calm in the Mediterranean with structured safety and coaching.

Equalization & depth

Build cleaner Frenzel mechanics, better timing and a calmer descent instead of compensating with force as pressure increases.

Freefall & efficiency

Develop body position, buoyancy awareness and the transition from active descent to freefall while staying connected to the dive plan.

Training focus

  • Equalization and descent timing
  • Freefall and line technique
  • Finning efficiency
  • Turn technique and recovery
  • Mental routines and relaxation
  • Active safety and buddy procedures

Conditions and progression

Depth sessions depend on sea conditions, visibility and the diver’s certification and current training level. Targets are adapted to the day; safe, repeatable technique matters more than forcing a number.
