Freediving community in Barcelona

Train with the same group every week after your course.

Freediving is safest with people who know you. Our community gives you regular sessions and a buddy you trust.

Join the WhatsApp community

Three steps from joining to your first session.

  1. Join the group

    Ask to join the WhatsApp community. It is where everything is shared.

  2. See this week's session

    Each weekly session is posted in the group with its time and place.

  3. Train with a buddy

    You always dive in pairs. Nobody in our community trains breath-holds alone.

Group events and trips are announced in the same group.

Members hear first about group dives, events and our winter trips with Shebl and Rimi.
