The method

Calm comes before depth in every lesson.

  • Relax first

    Breathing and relaxation come before any breath-hold. A calm body uses less oxygen.

  • Train your attention

    You learn to notice tension during a dive and let it go.

  • Make safety a habit

    Every dive has a buddy. You practise rescue until it is automatic.

  • Add depth when you are ready

    Depth increases only when your technique and comfort are in place.
