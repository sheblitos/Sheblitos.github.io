Why freediving

One breath teaches you to relax.

Underwater, in a new environment, everyone meets their own stress response. Freediving teaches you to stay relaxed in it, and to help each other do the same.

  1. BreatheSlow breathing settles the nervous system.
  2. HoldA calm breath-hold shows how you react under pressure.
  3. DecideClear, calm choices in an unfamiliar place.
  4. SupportBuddies help each other find their calm.

Trust

Trust you can't build in a meeting room

In the water, we build a calm, safe space together. Everyone shows care, gives trust and shares one goal: the deepest state of relaxation. Titles and departments fade away.

Better communicationReading each other without words, and speaking clearly when it counts.Stronger trustBonds built by supporting each other in the water.Calm under pressureA simple way to settle the body, in a dive or a deadline.

The day

What you actually do

  1. Dry, breathe & regulateWe start on the mat: breathwork, relaxation and down-regulating on command.
  2. Pool, in pairsRelaxed breath-holds, partner drills and communication under a little pressure.
  3. Open water & debriefAn optional, fully guided dive, then how to use it on Monday.

No one sits it out: every part of the day has a dry equivalent. Led personally by Shebl & Rimi.

Good to know

Questions people ask first

Do we need any freediving experience?

None at all. Every experience starts dry, on the mat, and builds gently from there.

What about non-swimmers or people nervous in water?

They're fully included. Every part of the day has a dry equivalent, so anyone can take part and get the same lessons.

Is it safe?

Yes. Every session is led in person by Shebl, an AIDA Instructor Trainer, with strict buddy and safety protocols, shallow controlled water and small groups.

Can this be a one-day experience or a retreat?

Both. We run full-day team experiences (morning to afternoon) and multi-day retreats in Barcelona or abroad.
