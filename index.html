Good to know

Questions before you start

Do I need any experience?

No. The Beginner course is made for complete beginners. You only need to be comfortable in the water.

Is freediving safe?

Yes, when it is taught properly. Every session is led by an AIDA Instructor Trainer, in small groups, with strict buddy and safety rules from day one.

Where do the courses take place?

In Barcelona: pool sessions first, then open-water dives in the Mediterranean.

Is equipment included?

Yes. All equipment is included in every course price. Bring a swimsuit and a towel.
