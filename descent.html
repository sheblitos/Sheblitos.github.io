More than a breath-hold

People come for the depth. They stay for what it does to the rest of their life.

Calm

One breath underwater drops you into deep calm. A real reset for daily stress.

Focus

Impulse control and a steady mind under pressure. It carries back to dry land.

Confidence

Comfort and safety in the water, so you can quietly push your limits.

Fitness

Breath, mobility and lung capacity. Low impact and easy on the joints.

The ocean, unlocked

Better snorkelling, spearfishing, scuba and surfing. Every water activity.

Belonging

Laughter on the boat, dinner under the stars. You leave with a community.

How it works

From the city to the sea.

Never held your breath underwater? Perfect. Three steps: land, pool, then the open sea.

1On land

Dry masterclass

Breathing, equalisation and your first breath-hold. Most people surprise themselves with how long they can go.

2In the pool

Pool freedive

Shallow, calm and fully guided, until one breath underwater feels completely natural.

3In the sea

Into the deep

A buoy, a line and everything you've trained. Arrive ready, and depth is the easy part.

The mammalian dive reflex

Your body already knows how.

You were born with it. Underwater, your heart slows and your body starts saving oxygen. You don't build the reflex. You learn to let it work, and that starts with a calm mind.
