Expedition 01 · Dahab, Egypt

Dahab

"The Blue Hole. The Desert. The Beginning."

A laid-back Sinai town: desert at dusk, the Red Sea at 24°C year-round, and the Blue Hole dropping past 90m just offshore, one of diving's wildest feelings.

Red Sea · 24°C year-round Blue Hole 90m+ Three dive sites Mount Moses & monastery Any water level
Enquire, Dahab What's included

The journey

01

The Lighthouse

The warm-up. Most sessions start here: easy access for every level, a training area just a swim from the shore, and a reef shelf that surrounds you with marine life.

A swim from shoreAll levelsReef shelf

02

The Blue Hole & The Bells

The famous one. We dive the huge coral walls of the Blue Hole and in through The Bells arch, one of the most iconic routes in freediving. Stunning at any depth.

The Bells arch25–28mDrops past 90m

03

Ras Mohamed

A full day at one of the best dive sites in the world. Fun dives around the Shark Observatory, the Yolanda wreck, and coral walls that genuinely look like screensavers.

Full-day tripShark ObservatoryYolanda wreck

04

Saint Catherine & Mount Moses

The other half of Sinai. A night hike up Mount Moses to meet the sunrise at the summit, and Saint Catherine's Monastery in the valley below it.

Sunrise from the summitNight hikeSaint Catherine's Monastery

Accommodation: Tranquilo Hostel, Dahab · Site briefings before every session · Plenty of bonuses along the way that never fit on an itinerary: desert dinners, Bedouin tea by the fire, and whatever the week turns up.

Expedition 02 · Bali → Labuan Bajo

Bali

"A journey across the Indonesian archipelago."

Bali to Labuan Bajo, 600km east. Ubud temples, world-class freediving, and a 4-day liveaboard with guaranteed mantas and whale sharks, ending at Komodo.

600km east 4-day liveaboard Mantas guaranteed Whale sharks guaranteed Any water level

01

Ubud

Start here. Temples, rice terraces, morning markets and good food. Bali at its most Balinese, before the islands take over.

TemplesRice terracesMorning markets

02

Amed

Freediving on the quiet northeast coast with Ocean Prana Bali. Ten minutes away, the USAT Liberty wreck at Tulamben: 30m of WWII steel swallowed whole by reef.

Gili Air

No motorbikes, no noise, no rush, diving with Pacific Freedive Academy. Turtles on every dive. The kind of island that makes you cancel your flight home.

Pacific Freedive AcademyTurtles on every diveCar-free island

The liveaboard

Four days aboard with Dewi, through the richest water of the whole trip. Oceanic mantas and whale sharks, both guaranteed.

4 days aboardMantas guaranteedWhale sharks guaranteed

05

Komodo & Labuan Bajo

Where the boat ends up. Padar Island at sunrise, Pink Beach, and Komodo dragons on Rinca, before you step off at Labuan Bajo.

Padar at sunrisePink BeachKomodo dragons

Local partners: Ocean Prana Bali (Amed) · Pacific Freedive Academy (Gili Air) · Dewi (liveaboard). Full logistics between every stop.

Expedition 04 · Tenerife, Canary Islands

Tenerife

"Europe's wild Atlantic edge."

Four hours from Europe: 21°C water, 40m visibility, resident pilot whales year-round, and Teide towering at 3,718m. The easiest trip to say yes to.

4 hours from Europe 21°C water 40m visibility Pilot whales guaranteed All levels welcome
Enquire, Tenerife What's included

The journey

01

Pilot whales

Guaranteed. A resident pod lives year-round in the southwest channel. Swimming alongside pilot whales in open Atlantic water is as wild as it sounds.

Guaranteed year-roundResident podOpen Atlantic

02

The Atlantic

Volcanic reef structures, 40m visibility and more marine life than anyone expects from Europe. December to March, the angel sharks arrive, one of the last significant populations left in the Atlantic.

40m visibilityVolcanic reefAngel sharks Dec–Mar

03

Teide

Spain's highest point at 3,718m, and a UNESCO site. Up through the crater by day, then stargazing above the cloud line, some of the clearest night sky in Europe.

3,718mUNESCO craterStargazing above the clouds

04

Anaga & Masca

The other half of the island. Ancient laurel forest in the Anaga range, the walk down Masca gorge, and black sand beaches to finish on.

Ancient laurel forestMasca gorgeBlack sand beaches

Local partner: Atlantis Freediving, Tabaiba · Pilot whales: guaranteed year-round · Angel sharks: December–March.

Guided freediving with Shebl on every dive, all dive equipment, accommodation and trusted local operators at each stop, and curated land adventures, desert dinners, volcano hikes, safaris, ancient ruins.

No certification required. Expeditions welcome experienced freedivers, complete beginners, and anyone who is comfortable in the water. The trips are not performance-focused, they are about exploring extraordinary places. Shebl briefs every dive and makes sure you're diving at whatever depth feels right.

Dahab, Egypt, the Blue Hole, Ras Mohamed reef, the Lighthouse, and desert dinners in Wadi Genai. Bali, Indonesia, Amed, Gili Air, and a 4-day liveaboard to Labuan Bajo with mantas and whale sharks. Sri Lanka, blue whale snorkelling in Mirissa, surfing, Sigiriya, and Yala safari. Tenerife, Spain, year-round pilot whales, angel sharks, and Teide at 3,718m.

Every expedition is led personally by Shebl and Rimi, the founders of Freediving Brain. Shebl is the AIDA Instructor Trainer in the water on every dive; Rimi runs the journey end to end. Together they've personally vetted every local operator and stay at each destination. There's no substitute team.
