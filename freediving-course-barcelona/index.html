What you'll learn

From your first calm breath-hold
to your first dive to depth.

  • 01

    Breathing & relaxation. Slow down your body and mind before every dive.

  • 02

    Breath-hold. Longer, calmer holds in the pool.

  • 03

    Equalisation. Clear your ears comfortably as you go down.

  • 04

    Finning & technique. Move efficiently and save oxygen.

  • 05

    Safety & buddy system. How freedivers look after each other.

  • 06

    Open water. Calm, guided dives around 20 m.

Good to know

Questions people ask first.

Do I need any experience?

No. The course is made for beginners. You just need to be comfortable in the water.

What happens in the free online session?

It's a live session with your instructor. You learn the basics of breath-hold, relaxation, equalisation and safety, and you can ask anything before deciding.

What if I don't want to continue?

Then you don't pay anything. The online session is free, with no commitment.

Where does the course take place?

In Barcelona: pool sessions first, then open-water dives in the Mediterranean.

How deep will I go?

The AIDA 2 course works towards dives of around 20 m, always at your own pace and comfort.

Is equipment included?

Yes, all equipment is included in the course price.
