Freediving community in Barcelona

Freediving is built on trust. The goal of the Barcelona community is simple: help people meet reliable buddies, train together, learn from each other and keep the sport social as well as safe.

Find buddies

Connect with other freedivers for pool sessions, open-water dives and shared training. Nobody should need to practise breath-hold diving alone.

Train together

Community members can connect around dry practice, stretching, pool training and Mediterranean sessions according to certification and experience.

Start freediving

Curious but not certified yet? Join the community, meet people in the sport and explore a beginner AIDA course when you are ready.

Why community matters in freediving

A strong buddy culture is more than a social benefit. Freediving safety depends on people knowing each other, communicating clearly and taking active responsibility for the diver they are watching. We want Barcelona to have a community where people recognise each other on the pool deck and at the buoy—not a collection of strangers who only meet during courses.

Freediving in Barcelona

Barcelona gives the community access to indoor pool training and Mediterranean open water. Conditions, depth and access vary with the day, so local knowledge and good buddy communication matter.
