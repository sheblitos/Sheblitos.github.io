Getting started

Do I need experience to start?

None at all. The Beginner course (AIDA 1 + AIDA 2) is designed for complete beginners: you only need basic comfort in water. All equipment is provided, in small groups with personal attention.

I can barely hold my breath for 30 seconds. Can I still do this?

Yes, and that's exactly where most students start. The urge to breathe is triggered by CO₂, not lack of oxygen, and your mind can learn to work with it. By the end of the first pool session most students hold their breath for well over two minutes.

Is freediving safe?

Freediving is safe when practised with proper training, a qualified buddy and the right protocols, all covered from your first course. Every session is supervised by an AIDA Instructor Trainer, and we teach you to never dive alone.

Courses

What is an AIDA freediving course?

An internationally recognised certification covering breath-hold technique, safety, equalisation and the mental game. We offer the Beginner course (AIDA 1 + AIDA 2), AIDA 3, AIDA 4 and the Instructor course, in Barcelona's pool and the Mediterranean.

How long does each course take?

Beginner (AIDA 1 + AIDA 2): 3.5 days. AIDA 3: 3 days. AIDA 4: 4 days. Instructor: 10 to 21 days. All include equipment and AIDA certification on completion.

How deep do you dive?

The Beginner course works towards around 20 m, AIDA 3 around 30 m and AIDA 4 around 40 m. Depth is always a consequence of calm, never the goal itself.

Are AIDA certifications recognised internationally?

Yes. They are accepted worldwide at dive sites, liveaboards and freediving schools, and they do not expire.

I have a certificate from another school. Can I continue here?

Yes. Your certificate transfers directly and you start from your next level. Certified freedivers can also join our training sessions.

Our method

What is the Barcelona Freedivers method?

It uses the mammalian dive response, the body's built-in reaction to immersion, together with structured breathing and nervous-system regulation. Depth and breath-hold time are by-products of calm, not willpower.
